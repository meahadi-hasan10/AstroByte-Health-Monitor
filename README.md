## Problem

Astronauts on long-duration space missions experience unique health risks caused by microgravity, radiation exposure, isolation, disrupted sleep cycles, stress, and other environmental factors.

Continuous monitoring of astronauts' physiological and behavioral health data is important for identifying potential health risks early and supporting timely decision-making during space missions.

## Proposed Solution

**AstroByte Health Monitor** is a web-based astronaut health monitoring platform designed to collect, visualize, and analyze health-related data.

The system will provide an easy-to-understand dashboard for monitoring astronaut health indicators and will use AI-assisted analysis to identify unusual patterns, potential health risks, and changes in an astronaut's condition.

## Planned Features

- Astronaut health dashboard
- Heart rate monitoring
- Blood oxygen monitoring
- Body temperature monitoring
- Sleep monitoring
- Stress and fatigue indicators
- Radiation exposure information
- Historical health data visualization
- Health trend analysis
- Abnormal condition detection
- AI-powered health insights
- Risk alerts and warning system
- Mission health summary
- Responsive web interface

## AI Usage

Artificial Intelligence will be used to analyze astronaut health data and identify unusual patterns or potential health risks.

Planned AI capabilities include:

- Health trend analysis
- Anomaly detection
- Risk-level estimation
- AI-generated health insights
- Early warning support
- Personalized health recommendations

AI will be used as a decision-support component rather than as a replacement for professional medical assessment.
